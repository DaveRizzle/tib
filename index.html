Questions

Before you book, you're probably wondering…

What actually happens on the discovery call? +

A relaxed 20-minute conversation, with no slide deck and no hard sell. We talk through your goals, your training history and what's been getting in the way, and figure out together whether coaching is the right move for you right now.

Do I need to be advanced or already strong? +

No. The whole approach is built around your current numbers and starting point. Everything scales to you, whether you're rebuilding a base or chasing a specific lift.

Is this online, in person, or both? +

Programming and the app are fully remote, so you can train wherever you are.

What is the app, and does it cost extra? +

It's the system you'll train inside: your sessions, prescriptions and strength tracking in one place, on web, iOS and Android. Access comes as part of your coaching. There's no separate consumer signup.

How much time per week does it take? +

We design the plan around the time you realistically have, not an ideal you can't sustain.

What does coaching cost? +

Strength-only coaching is typically equivalent to $400–$500 per month, depending on the coaching period. Period-specific bundle discounts are available. Book a free discovery call and we’ll talk through the options that fit your goals.
