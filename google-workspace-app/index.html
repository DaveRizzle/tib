Operator and intended users

The Google consent-screen application named The Infinite Body is operated by David Robertson, trading as The Infinite Body, the strength-coaching business behind www.theinfinitebody.com. The business contact is admin@theinfinitebody.com.

The app consists of operator-run scripts and AI-assisted business workflows. Its intended user is the business operator using the business Google Workspace account. Google connections are authorised through Google's OAuth consent flow. This public information page is available without signing in; there is no public account-registration or Google-connection form here.

These tools help operate the coaching business and improve its website. Coaching clients and readers can use the public website without granting the app access to their Google accounts.

What the app does

  • Search and website reporting: inspect Search Console properties, indexing and sitemap status, and report search queries, impressions, clicks, pages, countries and devices. Google Analytics reports help the operator understand website visits, engagement and recorded enquiry or booking signals.
  • Organic SEO keyword research: use Google Ads account/reporting data and request keyword ideas and historical search-demand metrics to prioritise useful website content. Keyword-planning API access depends on Google's approval of the project's access level.
  • Workspace productivity and administration: support operator-directed work with business email, files, calendars, contacts, spreadsheets, documents, presentations and forms, together with Workspace users, groups, organisational units, domains, customer settings, roles and audit/usage reports.

The research scripts use reporting and research endpoints. The existing app also has broader permissions that can allow sending, editing, deleting or administering business information. The Google Ads permission can permit campaign changes; it is not a read-only permission. Advertising delivery and increases in advertising spend require the operator's separate, explicit approval for the specific action.

Google data and why it is accessed

Access depends on the permissions granted by the Google account and the workflow the operator runs. The existing business authorisation includes:

  • Google account identityEmail address and account identity, to check that business work uses the intended Google account.
  • Search Console, Analytics and Google AdsSite properties and search performance; website analytics and configuration information; accessible Ads accounts, campaign/reporting information, search terms and keyword-demand metrics, for business reporting and organic search research.
  • Gmail, Drive and Workspace contentMessages and attachments, files and their metadata, calendar events, contacts, spreadsheet and document content, presentations and forms, for operator-directed communication, scheduling and document work. Granted permissions include read and write capabilities.
  • Workspace administration and reportsDirectory and organisational information, administrator roles, audit events and usage information, for administering the business Workspace.

Existing reporting also uses a separate service account for Analytics and a separate Calendar booking integration. That integration reads selected event metadata, stores a booking register in Cloudflare, and sends pseudonymous booking/cancellation signals to Google Analytics. It does not send attendee names, email addresses, event titles or notes in those Analytics payloads. It is not a new permission requested by this page.

Report outputs and selected business content can be processed in AI-assisted operator workflows. See the processor and AI disclosure and the full technical scope list before authorising access.

Support, revocation and deletion requests

For questions about this Google app, the data it uses or a deletion request, email admin@theinfinitebody.com. You can review and remove its Google-account connection at Google Account connections.

Removing a connection stops future access through that authorisation. It does not automatically delete existing reports, Google-hosted files, AI conversations or records held by a separately authorised service account. The Google-data privacy disclosure explains storage, retention, sharing and the manual deletion process.
