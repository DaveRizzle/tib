## David's methodology to preserve

- Consultation with the client about their unique context and stressors is central.
- Training logs, check-ins and optional wearable signals are inputs to that
  consultation; no dashboard or score determines the decision alone.
- Review recovery context and average training load.
- Compare external demand (work prescribed/completed) with internal response.
- Evaluate trends across an appropriate short cycle, quarter or annual plan.
- Trainees commonly change routines too quickly and may overlook missed meals or poor
  sleep.
- David uses planned, undulating variation, but public copy should explain the concept
  in plain language before using technical terminology.
- HRV alone must not be described as a formal measure of allostatic load.
