# Commercial Homepage Brief

**URL:** `https://www.theinfinitebody.com/`  
**Page ID:** HOME  
**Status:** Existing commercial owner; improve without creating a duplicate service page  
**Market focus:** United States  
**Primary conversion:** Confirmed discovery-call booking

## Search intent ownership

**Primary query hypothesis:** `online strength coaching`

**Secondary language, not separate pages:**

- online strength coach;
- remote strength coaching;
- online strength trainer;
- personalized strength training program;
- custom strength program.

The homepage is the sole commercial category owner while The Infinite Body offers one
primary service. Do not create `/online-strength-coaching/` unless the business later
has a page architecture that gives HOME and the service page genuinely different jobs.

## Audience hypothesis

An experienced or committed strength trainee whose work, travel, schedule or changing
recovery makes generic fixed programs difficult to sustain. “Busy professionals” is the
current broad framing. “Executives” remains an unvalidated subsegment and should not be
inserted as an SEO claim.

## Customer job

Help me continue making useful strength progress without personally redesigning my
training whenever my schedule, equipment or performance changes.

## Failed alternatives

- random programs found online;
- static PDFs and spreadsheets;
- generic templates that assume stable weeks;
- self-programming without a clear progression model;
- app-only plans without coaching judgement.

## Offer mechanism

The page may describe mechanisms already visible and supportable:

- current goals and strength baseline;
- periodized phases;
- loads derived from the client's real maxes;
- set-by-set delivery and logging in the app;
- progression trends;
- coach review and adjustment;
- a discovery call to assess fit.

Do not imply that software alone adapts the program if a coach makes the decision.

## Required trust elements

- David Robertson identified visibly as the Online Strength Coach;
- accurate coaching-history statement;
- visible, permissioned testimonials;
- clear remote-delivery explanation;
- pricing currency stated as USD;
- honest qualification language and no medical positioning;
- privacy and direct contact routes.

## Conversion hierarchy

1. Primary CTA: book a free discovery call.
2. Secondary CTA: understand how the coaching/app works.
3. Contact alternative: public email.

Measurement hierarchy:

- page views and scrolling are engagement;
- `booking_click` is intent;
- `appointment_booked` is a confirmed Calendar booking;
- qualification, attendance, payment and retention are downstream outcomes.

## Page structure

1. Brand-led opening and category/value framing.
2. Main service hero with one document-level H1.
3. Credibility/mechanism strip.
4. Problem and failed alternatives.
5. Three-step coaching process.
6. App/mechanism evidence.
7. Coach and method.
8. Permissioned client evidence.
9. FAQs, including remote delivery and USD pricing.
10. Discovery-call CTA and contact alternative.

## Copy and SEO constraints

- Use US spelling for the US-focused commercial page.
- Keep one H1. Supporting visual hero language must use lower-level headings.
- Preserve one absolute canonical to the `www` homepage.
- Structured data must match visible copy.
- Do not force executive, injury, rehabilitation or guaranteed-result language.
- Do not add ratings or unsupported credentials.
- Do not link to proposed pages until they are published and pass their gates.
- Avoid repeating “personalized,” “adaptive” and “data-driven” without explaining how.

## Evidence gates for deeper repositioning

The following changes remain blocked until more evidence exists:

- executive-specific headline or page;
- major busy-professional promise beyond current framing;
- claims that travel/schedule adaptation is a unique differentiator;
- detailed performance outcome claims;
- separate commercial service page;
- major keyword-led title changes.

Required evidence includes US result-page review, Ads volume, first-party JTBD language,
qualified bookings and relevant permissioned case studies.

## Safe implementation in this pass

The current evidence supports these low-risk corrections:

1. Keep one document-level H1 by making the brand-led “Lost your training marbles?”
   heading an H2 while preserving its visual treatment.
2. Identify David visibly as `David Robertson · Online Strength Coach` in the coach
   section.
3. State the variable monthly price explicitly in USD.
4. Standardize the homepage's visible and structured-data spelling to US English:
   `personalized` and `periodized`.
5. Preserve all current canonical, analytics, booking and structured-data semantics.

These corrections improve clarity and consistency without pretending the remaining
positioning hypotheses have been proven.
