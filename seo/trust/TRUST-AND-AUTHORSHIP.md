## Credentials policy

No qualification or certification is currently approved for publication. Do not infer a
credential from a job history, testimonial, skill or years of experience.

Before a credential is published, record:

1. exact credential name;
2. issuing organisation;
3. award date and current/expired status;
4. verification evidence;
5. whether the issuer permits public/logo use;
6. relevance to the claim or content topic;
7. the precise visible wording approved by David.

Do not place a credential only in structured data. Any structured credential must match
visible content.

## Authorship standard for future guides

Every substantive organic guide should display:

- `Written by David Robertson, Online Strength Coach`;
- original publication date;
- meaningful last-reviewed date only after real review;
- the intended audience and scope;
- references for factual research claims;
- a distinction between evidence, coaching judgement and client anecdote;
- a medical-scope statement where pain, injury or health conditions are discussed.

Do not update dates merely to imply freshness. Do not cite a source that was not read.
Do not imply clinical review unless a named qualified clinician actually reviewed it.

## Claim hierarchy

### Level 1 — Directly demonstrable

Suitable for confident visible use when current:

- app screenshots and documented app behaviour;
- the booking process;
- remote delivery;
- USD pricing range;
- set logging, prescribed sessions and visible programme phases;
- provider identity and public contact details.

### Level 2 — Provider method and experience

Suitable with clear attribution and accurate boundaries:

- coaching since 2008;
- personalized and periodized programming;
- loads based on client strength/max information;
- coach adjustment based on progress and context;
- data-informed coaching judgement.

Avoid turning these into guaranteed outcome claims.

### Level 3 — Client anecdote

May be used only with permission and context:

- testimonial statements;
- individual strength outcomes;
- changes in consistency, confidence or enjoyment;
- return-to-training experiences.

A testimonial is not general proof that every client will achieve the same outcome.

### Level 4 — Research or health claim

Requires a suitable source and careful scope:

- injury risk or prevention;
- pain, rehabilitation or medical-condition claims;
- population-level performance effects;
- statistical outcome claims;
- nutritional/health claims.

The Infinite Body is strength coaching, not diagnosis or medical treatment. Refer
clinical questions to an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

## Case-study publication gate

A detailed case study requires:

- written permission covering name/image/quote and intended channels;
- an option to anonymize or withdraw where agreed;
- source records for baseline and outcome;
- time period and relevant context;
- clear description of what coaching did and did not provide;
- client review of the final draft;
- no unnecessary health, employment or other personal information;
- no guarantee that another client will reproduce the outcome.

Use `CASE-STUDY-TEMPLATE.md`. Do not create `/client-results/` until multiple substantive
permissioned cases exist.
